= Organizations API
:toc:
:toclevels: 3

== Overview
Endpoints for managing GitHub organizations, members, teams, webhooks, roles and more.

== API Interfaces

=== OrgApi — Organization Management
[cols="1,2,3", options="header"]
|===
| Method | Endpoint | Description
| GET | `/organizations` | List organizations
| GET | `/user/orgs` | List orgs for authenticated user
| GET | `/users/{username}/orgs` | List orgs for a user
| GET | `/orgs/{org}` | Get an organization
| PATCH | `/orgs/{org}` | Update an organization
| DELETE | `/orgs/{org}` | Delete an organization
| GET | `/orgs/{org}/members` | List organization members
| GET | `/orgs/{org}/members/{username}` | Check organization membership for a user
| DELETE | `/orgs/{org}/members/{username}` | Remove an organization member
| GET/PUT/DELETE | `/orgs/{org}/memberships/{username}` | Get/Set/Remove org membership
| GET/PATCH | `/user/memberships/orgs` | List/Update org memberships for authenticated user
| GET | `/orgs/{org}/public_members` | List public org members
| PUT/DELETE | `/orgs/{org}/public_members/{username}` | Set/Remove public org membership
| GET | `/orgs/{org}/outside_collaborators` | List outside collaborators
| PUT/DELETE | `/orgs/{org}/outside_collaborators/{username}` | Convert/Remove outside collaborator
| GET | `/orgs/{org}/invitations` | List pending invitations
| POST | `/orgs/{org}/invitations` | Create an invitation
| DELETE | `/orgs/{org}/invitations/{invitation_id}` | Cancel invitation
| GET | `/orgs/{org}/invitations/{invitation_id}/teams` | List invitation teams
| GET | `/orgs/{org}/failed_invitations` | List failed invitations
| GET/PUT/DELETE | `/orgs/{org}/blocks/{username}` | Block/Unblock users
| GET | `/orgs/{org}/credential-authorizations` | List SAML SSO authorizations
| DELETE | `/orgs/{org}/credential-authorizations/{credential_id}` | Remove SAML SSO authorization
| GET | `/orgs/{org}/audit-log` | Get the audit log
| GET/POST | `/orgs/{org}/hooks` | List/Create webhooks
| GET/PATCH/DELETE | `/orgs/{org}/hooks/{hook_id}` | Get/Update/Delete webhook
| POST | `/orgs/{org}/hooks/{hook_id}/pings` | Ping webhook
| GET | `/orgs/{org}/hooks/{hook_id}/deliveries` | List webhook deliveries
| GET/POST | `…/deliveries/{delivery_id}/attempts` | Get/Redeliver delivery
| GET/POST/GET/PATCH/DELETE | `/orgs/{org}/custom_roles…` | Custom roles management
|===
*Models:* `OrgMembership`, `OrgInvitation`, `CredentialAuthorization`, `CustomRole`, `UpdateOrgRequest`

=== OrgTeamApi — Teams & Discussions
[cols="1,2,3", options="header"]
|===
| Method | Endpoint | Description
| GET/POST | `/orgs/{org}/teams` | List/Create teams
| GET/PATCH/DELETE | `/orgs/{org}/teams/{team_slug}` | Get/Update/Delete team
| GET | `/orgs/{org}/teams/{team_slug}/members` | List team members
| GET/PUT/DELETE | `/orgs/{org}/teams/{team_slug}/memberships/{username}` | Manage team membership
| GET | `/orgs/{org}/teams/{team_slug}/repos` | List team repos
| GET/PUT/DELETE | `/orgs/{org}/teams/{team_slug}/repos/{owner}/{repo}` | Team repo permissions
| GET | `/orgs/{org}/teams/{team_slug}/teams` | List child teams
| GET/POST | `/orgs/{org}/teams/{team_slug}/discussions` | List/Create discussions
| GET/PATCH/DELETE | `/orgs/{org}/teams/{team_slug}/discussions/{discussion_number}` | Manage discussions
| GET/POST | `…/discussions/{discussion_number}/comments` | List/Create discussion comments
| GET/PATCH/DELETE | `…/comments/{comment_number}` | Manage comments
| GET | `/user/teams` | List teams for authenticated user
|===
*Models:* `TeamMembership`, `TeamDiscussion`, `TeamDiscussionComment`, `CreateTeamRequest`, `UpdateTeamRequest`
